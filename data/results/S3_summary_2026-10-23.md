## What we learned
1. **Late on expiry day, options are priced above the moves they deliver.** Buyers lose; sellers win often. But the
   loss on a bad afternoon wipes out months of gains. It happens only on expiry days: on other days the same trade
   loses.
2. **That test year was unusually calm.** On 2021-24 expiry days the same late sale would have lost about 28 % of the
   premium on average (estimated from VIX).
3. **Plain option selling doesn't pay.** The weekly straddle sold to expiry lost 7.2 % a trade over 2021-24. Selling
   far puts every month won 34 months in a row, then one month (April 2025) cost 7.5 times the premium.
4. **The VIX rule of thumb holds:** VIX overstates the next week's move, except when VIX is above 22 and rising. Avoid
   selling then.
5. **Fees matter at 1 lot:** ₹20 an order is fixed, so a 1-lot fly loses 5-17 % of its premium to fees.
6. Round numbers and "max pain" do not pull the expiry close.
