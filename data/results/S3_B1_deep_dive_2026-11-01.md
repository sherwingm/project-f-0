# S3 B1 · breakout + volume: where it loses (deep dive, 2026-11-01)

Descriptive only: no rule changed, nothing picked from it. Primary cell `OR 15 min · volume 2x · stop 25 %`, 488 trades, 2024-10-01 → 2026-10-28, NIFTY, spot store after the 2026-10-30 repair.

## 1. The index itself barely follows through
- Median opening range (09:15-09:29) 0.35 % of NIFTY.
- NIFTY's move in the breakout direction after the signal, median: 15 min +0.005 %, 60 min +0.016 %, to 15:15 +0.048 % (mean +0.011 %).
- Ends 15:15 in the breakout direction on 53 % of days (a coin flip).
- Closes back inside the range before 15:15 on 435 of 488 days; median 5 min after the signal; within 30 min on 348.
- Net when NIFTY ended in the direction: +11.1 % (260 trades); when not: -18.1 %.

## 2. The volume test hardly filters
- First breakout with no volume test: 495 trades, -2.8 % a trade; with 2x volume: 488, -2.5 %.
- Same signal minute on 330 of 487 common days; otherwise the confirmed minute is usually 1-20 min later.

## 3. Exits
| Exit | Trades | Mean net % | Median | Win rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| stop | 86 | -28.2 % | -27.4 % | 0 % |
| time | 103 | +28.6 % | +15.9 % | 73 % |
| trail | 299 | -5.9 % | -15.7 % | 24 % |

- 228 of 299 trail exits are losses (158 at -15 % or worse): the trail arms on any profit, so a trade that is up 1 % and then falls exits near -25 %, like a stop.
- Stops book about -28 %, not -25 %: exit slippage, the minute's move past the level and charges.
- Wins 146, average +36.6 %; losses 342, average -19.3 %. Break-even at a 30 % win rate needs the average win near 2.3x the average loss; it is 1.9x.

## 4. Max up while held
- Quartiles +5 %, +14 %, +36 %; never above +5 %: 123 trades; reached +20 % and still booked a loss: 61.
- Median 30 min from entry to the peak; peak within 10 min on 159.
