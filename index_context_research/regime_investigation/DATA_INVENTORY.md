# Data inventory (coordinator, 17 Nov 2026)

Consolidates Agent 1's inventory (`interim/agent1_inventory/INVENTORY.md` and `inventory.csv`, 55 variables x 13 columns, with its check scripts and hashes) and the approved downloads of 17 Nov (`DATA_DOWNLOAD_MANIFEST.json`, `DOWNLOAD_QUALITY_CHECKS.md`). Nothing here was downloaded by the agents; the download phase ran before them under the user's approval. Missing stays missing.

## Rule for "known before entry"
G1 / G1-F / G1-C enter between about 09:30 and 13:00 IST (the minute after the signal minute); XP enters at 09:16 IST. A variable is a pre-entry predictor only if it was published before that minute. Publication clock times are NOT stated inside the NSE, NSE IX and third-party files; the rules below are the documented market conventions (evening publication after the close of the session described), stated as a limitation.

## By variable family
- **NIFTY, NIFTY Bank, SENSEX spot 1-minute:** `../fo-index-data/data/index/spot_1m/`, 2021-10-20 to 2026-10-28; aligned by timestamp. The minute-bar stamp convention (start vs end of minute) was not verified. Spot after 2026-10-29 is not in the store. 3 weekend special sessions (2024-01-20, 2025-02-01, 2026-02-01) exist in the minute store but not in the NSE index file.
- **NIFTY daily closes:** `data/eod2/nifty 50.csv` (2012-2026) equals the new NSE all-index file exactly on 2,188 days. Used for the direction regimes (boundaries from 2018-01-01 to 2024-09-30).
- **India VIX:** minute and daily in the store, plus the NSE index file. **The raw cached minute VIX for 2021-10-20 to 2023-08-22 is corrupt (about 17 % of rows x100 or /100 on 455 days); `index_data.load.vix_1m` repairs it.** `vix_daily.parquet` ends 2026-10-23 with a NaN close; 13 days lack a daily reference. One residual repaired-series row (2025-04-07 09:15).
- **GIFT NIFTY (new):** `external_data/gift_bhavcopy_futures.csv`, NSE IX index-futures bhavcopy, 867 trading days from 2023-07-03 (no row on 2025-01-01, 2026-01-01 and the Budget weekends). Row of date D: `open` = first trade of the GIFT session starting 06:30 IST of D (known before entry); `prev_settle` is NOT the previous row's settlement (it already contains US-hours movement); high / low / close / settlement / volume of row D are NOT known before entry. Near-month open interest is empty. **The 09:00 level, intraday and pre-open history do not exist in the data.** Definition used as primary: open(D) / settlement of the same expiry on the previous GIFT trade date - 1 (correlation with the actual NIFTY 09:15 gap 0.75; the `prev_settle` column version only 0.50).
- **Opening gaps / intraday behaviour:** derivable from the spot minutes; used: opening gap, opening range width, signal-minute move.
- **FII / DII cash flows (new, limited):** `external_data/fii_dii_cash_stockezee.csv`, third-party archive, 218 days from 2025-12-01 to 2026-11-17, **September 2026 missing at source**, verified against NSE's own combined table on one day only (2026-11-17). Covers only 59 of 154 G1-F trades (102 of 259 G1, 96 of 240 G1-C, 43 of 108 XP). **Nothing before Dec 2025 exists locally.** Descriptive use only.
- **Participant-wise derivatives positioning:** `g1_research/data/external/participant_oi/`, 1,280 daily files 2021-09-01 to 2026-11-06, clean (no gaps, header dates match). Contains FII, DII, Client, Pro; index and stock futures long / short; index options call / put long / short. Published after the close of the session it describes. Earlier studies used only the FII index-futures long share.
- **NIFTY futures price / OI:** `options_daily/` bhavcopy (2018-2026, daily). No futures minutes. GIFT carries no usable OI.
- **Options:** NIFTY option minutes 2024-08-02 to 2026-10-28 with OI (about 49 % of rows are zero-volume fillers, OI is 0 on about 16 % of sampled rows); SENSEX misses 2024-09-09 to 09-12; no BANKNIFTY option minutes; `expiries.parquet` holds only future expiries (history from folder names or bhavcopy).
- **US and Asian markets (new):** `external_data/yahoo/` daily from 2018 for Nikkei, Hang Seng, Shanghai, KOSPI, Taiwan, ASX200, Nasdaq, S&P 500, US VIX, dollar index, US 10-year yield, WTI, Brent, gold, USD/INR, NIFTY; hourly S&P / Nasdaq futures and USD/INR for two years (from 2024-11-17). **Timestamps are bar-open stamps; `local_offset_s` is one constant per file and misdates summer rows, so the UTC date of the stamp must be used (Agent 5 confirmed Agents 3b and 4 did this correctly).** US bar dated D closes 01:30-02:30 IST of D+1, so Indian session T uses US dates before T. Nikkei and KOSPI open 05:30 IST, Taiwan 06:30, Hang Seng and Shanghai 07:00: their opens are pre-entry, their closes fall inside the entry window.
- **USD/INR:** the Yahoo daily series is unusable (close equals open on 66 % of days; a London-day bar); the hourly series (from 2024-11-18) is used as an approximation of the previous Indian session (13 of 154 G1-F trades have no value).
- **Crude oil:** quality-limited: roll artefacts (Brent -14.5 % vs WTI -1.2 % on 2026-04-01) and daily vs hourly closes differing by more than 2 % on 27 of 527 days (flagged; robustness run without them). DXY has 466 empty weekend rows; US VIX has 82 empty holiday rows; ASX200's open equals the previous close on 65 % of days (unusable).
- **Market breadth (new):** `external_data/nse_breadth_daily.csv`, advances / declines / unchanged and turnover shares for every NSE equity (EQ series), 1,316 sessions from 2021-10 to 2026-11; real breadth, not the survivorship-biased proxy of the earlier study. 60 rows fall on NSE holidays (57 exact copies of the previous row; 3 unexplained: 2021-11-05, 2024-01-22, 2024-05-20) and the 2022-08-08 session is missing: key it to the NSE session calendar. Correlation of the advance share with that day's NIFTY return 0.73.
- **Sector / index performance (new):** `external_data/nse_index_closes.csv`: 181 NSE indices (sector, midcap, smallcap, VIX) daily 2018-01-01 to 2026-11-17; the two Budget weekend sessions are absent. Only NIFTY Bank minus NIFTY 50 was used as a sector measure.
- **News sentiment:** GDELT daily tone for "Nifty OR Sensex OR Indian stocks" (`g1_research/data/external/gdelt/`): one global machine-scored number a day, 1,850 days with gaps (2025: 348 of 365; 2026: 285 of 311); usable for the previous day only. No timestamped news archive exists.
- **Scheduled events:** `scanner/s3_buy4.py` lists RBI policy, Budget, FOMC and the 2024 US election from 2024-10; the lists stop at RBI 2026-10-07 and FOMC 2026-09-16. RBI (10:00) and Budget (11:00) announcements fall inside the entry window. No other election, CPI or GDP date exists locally and none was invented. `data/events.sqlite` is stock-level only.
- **Strategy trade records:** `g1_research/results/g1_live_replay_trades.csv` (G1 259, G1-F 154), `g1_research/data/g1c_opt_trades.csv` (G1-C 235 priced of 240), `g1_research/data/xp_history_minute.csv` (XP 107). History replays only. No BANKNIFTY / SENSEX option-trade records for these rules.
