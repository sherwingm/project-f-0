## Regime definitions (fixed now; boundaries come from NIFTY / VIX HISTORY 2018-01-01 to 2024-09-30, never from strategy returns)
- **Direction, known at the previous close.** ret20 = previous close / close 20 sessions earlier - 1 (NIFTY daily closes). Classes by quintiles of ret20 over the history above: strongly bearish (Q1), moderately bearish (Q2), sideways (Q3), moderately bullish (Q4), strongly bullish (Q5). For combinations the five collapse to three: bearish (Q1-Q2), sideways (Q3), bullish (Q4-Q5). Robustness measures (reported, not primary): ret5, ret10 quintiles; moving-average structure (bull = close > SMA20 > SMA50, bear = close < SMA20 < SMA50, else mixed).
- **Volatility.** Level: India VIX previous close, low < 25th, normal 25th-75th, high > 75th percentile of VIX daily closes (history above; repaired series only: `index_data.load.vix_1m` / `vix_daily.parquet`). Direction: VIX previous close vs 5 sessions earlier: rising >= +5 %, falling <= -5 %, flat otherwise. Sudden expansion: VIX at the signal minute >= 1.10 x the previous close.
- **Overnight.** (a) **True GIFT NIFTY pre-open movement: NOT available** (daily settlements only; no 09:00 level): not tested; the daily GIFT settlement proxies are reported only as clearly labelled proxies. (b) **Actual NIFTY opening gap** (09:15 open vs previous close, known before every entry): gap-down <= -0.3 %, flat between, gap-up >= +0.3 % (G1-F excludes gap >= +0.3 % by its own filter, so its gap-up cell is empty by construction). (c) Global overnight: S&P 500 previous US session return (known before the Indian open): down <= -0.3 %, flat, up >= +0.3 % (data ends 2026-09-25; later trades stay missing). (d) "Indicated vs actual opening": not testable.
- **Combinations (only these):** direction3 x volatility-level3; direction3 x gap class; volatility-level3 x gap class; direction3 x volatility x gap only if every cell has >= 10 trades, otherwise reported "insufficient".
- **Minimum cell size:** statements only for cells with n >= 10; cells with n < 10 are listed as "too few" and carry no conclusion.

## Tests (small family, pre-registered)
Single-condition hypotheses on G1-F, then the same on G1, G1-C, XP: H1 direction (5 classes), H2 VIX level (3), H3 VIX direction (3), H4 sudden expansion (yes / no), H5 opening gap class, H6 global overnight (3); combination hypotheses H7-H9 (the three pairs). Method: per cell n, wins, losses, win rate, mean and median net %, total Rs, average profit and loss, max up through the next close (MFE) and the largest adverse move (MAE, option minute close vs paid), top-5 % concentration; bootstrap 95 % intervals resampling trading days; Kruskal-Wallis across the classes of a condition and Welch difference between named classes; Benjamini-Hochberg 10 % across the family (9 hypotheses per strategy). Explanatory results (whole sample) and predictive results (rules above, discovery to 2025-09-30, then consistency in the second half and by half-year) are reported in separate sections. No machine learning, no threshold search, no re-cutting after seeing returns. Multiple trades on one date are clustered (G1-F has at most one trade a day).
**Anatomy fields for every G1-F trade:** entry date / time; NIFTY direction before entry (ret1, ret5, ret10, ret20); VIX level and change; overnight / gap; intraday trend from 09:15 to the signal minute; breadth (survivorship-biased proxy only); days to expiry; trade duration; MFE; MAE; exit reason; net % and net Rs.
**Information-time rule:** every variable's publication time is stated and compared with the entry minute; anything published after entry is excluded as a predictor. Missing stays missing. No downloads, no fabricated proxies; a proxy is allowed only when labelled.
