**Mission (user, 15-16 Nov):** find WHEN the existing index strategies (G1, G1-F, G1-C, XP, Near Tighty) trade well or badly using information known BEFORE entry; index only; no stock strategies; no change to any existing strategy, paper system, server or recorder.
**Warning given first (project rule "read solved research first"):** news tone, FII/DII positions, VIX regimes, G1 predictors (about 15 ideas) and ATM/ITM were already tested with no candidate (registry E-20261108-0x, E-20261109-0x). They are included here only to complete the unified table and are marked "repeat". Genuinely new: GIFT Nifty basis (fetched 16 Nov, `data/gift/`), index-futures price / OI conditions, option-chain OI / IV / skew at entry, event-window flags, US overnight move, yearly tables per strategy.
**Scope kept honest:** FINNIFTY, MIDCPNIFTY, BANKEX have no spot / option history here (futures settlements only) and are not tested. Real GIFT pre-open prices do not exist in the data (daily settlements only). FII/DII cash flows are not available historically. Emotional-discipline analysis is not possible (paper systems are automated; live logs are on the VM, which is out of bounds). Breadth is a survivorship-biased proxy (today's F&O list). All stated again in DATA_LIMITATIONS.md.

## Trade sets (existing files, definitions untouched)
- **G1** 259 NIFTY puts (`g1_research/results/g1_live_replay_trades.csv`, live rule: ATM, 50 % stop, next 15:29), **G1-F** = the 154 with `g1f == 1`, **G1-C** 240 NIFTY calls (`g1_research/data/g1c_opt_trades.csv`, column B), **XP** 107 expiry-day straddles (`g1_research/data/xp_history_minute.csv`, `hold_pct` = hold to 15:15). Near Tighty is the G1 subset 1-3 sessions to expiry (`dte`): reported as a G1 split, not a separate trade set. No SENSEX or BANKNIFTY option-trade history exists for these rules (SENSEX has max-up files only): "per index" analysis is limited to NIFTY and is said so.
- Window 2024-10-01 to 2026-10-28. **Discovery:** entry day <= 2025-09-30. **Validation:** later. No late period beyond that.

## Features (fixed now; each known before the entry minute; computed in `scripts/build_dataset.py`)
1 gap_pct (day open vs previous close); 2 vix_signal (India VIX at the signal minute); 3 vix_chg5 (VIX previous close vs 5 sessions earlier, %); 4 vix_pct1y (percentile of the previous close in the prior 250 sessions);
5 gift_basis (GIFT NIFTY near-month settlement of the previous session / NIFTY previous close - 1, %); 6 gift_basis_dev (gift_basis minus its 20-session mean); 7 gift_ret_prev (GIFT settlement change over the previous session, % -- a proxy, NOT the pre-open gap);
8 us_prev_ret (S&P 500 return of the last US session before the trade day; `data/parents/yf_GSPC.csv`, ends 2026-09-25, later days stay missing);
9 fut_oi_chg (NIFTY near-month future open-interest change on the previous session, %); 10 fut_px_chg (that future's price change, %); 11 fii_fut_longshare (FII index futures long / (long + short), previous session's participant file); 12 fii_fut_chg (its 1-session change);
13 atm_iv (Black-Scholes ATM implied volatility at the entry minute, call and put averaged, r = 6.5 %, no dividend); 14 pcr_oi (put OI / call OI over the 10 strikes each side of ATM at the entry minute); 15 iv_skew (put IV two strikes below ATM minus call IV two strikes above ATM, entry minute);
16 breadth_prev (share of the current F&O list closing up on the previous session; survivorship-biased proxy); 17 event_window (1 when the entry day or the next session is an RBI / Budget / FOMC date of `scanner/s3_buy4`); 18 expiry_day (entry day is a NIFTY expiry day).
Missing values stay missing (never zero); a feature with more than 30 % missing for a trade set is not tested for it.

## Tests
- **Stage 1 (individual factors):** for each trade set and feature, split at the DISCOVERY-period median into high / low. Report max up first (share >= +100 % where the file has it, mean, median), then n, win %, mean net % (1x), median, profit factor, total Rs, max drawdown, average win and loss, Welch t of (high - low), 95 % bootstrap interval of the difference. Benjamini-Hochberg at 10 % over all tested cells in discovery (two-sided p from t). Survivors are frozen to a file before validation is opened.
- **Validation (once):** a survivor passes only if the same side is better in validation, the difference has t >= 1.5 in the same direction, and the better side's mean net % is > 0.
- **Stage 2-4:** pairs only among features that passed validation; three-factor combinations only if the pair sample is >= 60 trades per cell; ablation (original, + A, + B, + A + B) for each pair. If fewer than 2 features pass, Stage 3 is reported "not run: nothing to combine".
- **Yearly / quarterly tables:** per trade set, per calendar year and quarter, plus weekday, expiry day vs not, gap tercile, VIX tercile, trending (previous close above SMA20) vs not, share of profit from the top 5 % of trades. 2026 is shown as a partial year.
- Verdict words per feature: FAILED, NO PREDICTIVE VALUE, PROMISING BUT INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE, PASSED DISCOVERY ONLY, PASSED OUT-OF-SAMPLE VALIDATION, REQUIRES LIVE PAPER VALIDATION.
- Samples are 107-259 trades and about 420+ earlier looks exist at this data: a pass can only be a lead for paper trading.
